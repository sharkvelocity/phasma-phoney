Phasma‑Phoney
Shark-Blades — This is my Text based envisioning of the ghost hunting game Phasmophobia.

Rules

- Correctly identify ghosts by behavior.
- Turn-based sanity, mimicry, and hunt danger.
- Each ghost has unique evidence & quirks.
- Mimic shows orbs and mimics others every few turns.
- Drop tools in any room; return to pick them up.
- Swap tools at van anytime (max 3 in hand).
- Crucifix and Smudge prevent hunts — once.
- Notebook and Lighter are always carried.

Select Your Loadout
