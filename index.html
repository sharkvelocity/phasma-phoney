Phasma‑Phoney
Shark-Blades — This game was an Envisionment of the first-person ghost hunting game Phasmophobia.

Rules

- Correctly identify ghosts by behavior.
- Turn system with sanity loss and hunt danger.
- Each ghost has unique evidence and personality.
- Some ghosts show signs only under specific conditions.
- The Mimic can show orbs visually but mimics other ghosts every few turns.
- You may only carry 3 tools at once (Notebook and Lighter excluded).
- Items can be dropped and returned to via the van.
- Use crucifix or smudge to avoid death during a hunt.

Select Your Loadout











You may select up to 3 items. Notebook and Lighter are always with you.
