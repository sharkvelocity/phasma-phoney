Phasma‑Phoney
Shark-Blades — This is my Text based envisioning of the ghost hunting game Phasmophobia.

Rules

- Use clues to identify the ghost.
- Choose 3 tools. Notebook + Lighter are always included.
- Ghosts may hunt when your sanity is low.
- The Mimic always shows orbs visually, but mimics another ghost's behavior every few turns.
- Use smudge sticks or crucifixes to survive hunts.
- Drop and pick up tools per room.
- Swap gear from van at any time.

Select Your Loadout

You may hold up to 3 tools. Notebook and Lighter are always with you.
