Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 4, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Phasma‑Phoney (“we”, “our”, or “us”). We are committed to protecting your privacy while you explore, play, and investigate within our game. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data and what your rights are.

2. Information We Collect

Phasma‑Phoney is designed to respect your privacy. We collect only the data necessary to operate and improve the game:

  • Non-Personal Information: This may include technical data such as your device type, browser, screen resolution, game performance metrics (like FPS), and general gameplay statistics (e.g., ghost guesses, evidence found, session duration).
  • No Personal Information: We do not collect or store your name, email, or any personally identifiable data unless you explicitly contact us for support.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Ensure the game runs smoothly on your device
  • Improve gameplay features and balance ghost behavior
  • Fix bugs or performance issues
  • Analyze anonymous trends for future updates

We do not sell, share, or rent your data to third parties. We do not serve ads.

4. Cookies and Analytics

Phasma‑Phoney does not use tracking cookies. If hosted via a third-party platform (such as GitHub Pages), basic analytics (like page visits) may be collected automatically by the platform for maintenance or security purposes.

We do not use Google Analytics or any third-party analytics tools that collect personal data.

5. Data Retention

We retain anonymous gameplay data temporarily during your session for the purpose of real-time gameplay. Once your session ends, any temporary data is discarded. We do not store player data persistently on a server.

6. Children’s Privacy

Phasma‑Phoney is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children. If you believe a child has interacted with us in a way that raises a privacy concern, please contact us and we will investigate and delete any applicable data.

7. Your Rights

Because Phasma‑Phoney does not collect or store personal data, most data privacy requests do not apply. However, if you reach out for support and provide your contact information, you have the right to:

  • Request deletion of your support message
  • Ask what information was shared and for what purpose

You can contact us at any time to make such requests.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the game evolves. Any updates will be posted in this document with a revised effective date. If we ever introduce account features or data storage, we’ll update this policy accordingly.
